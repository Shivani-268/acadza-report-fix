ABOUT THIS WORKSPACE

Designed for safe question repair

This console loads the supplied 43-question and 44-report dataset with precomputed triage recommendations. It does not call a live LLM. Taxonomy rules determine which repairs may be auto-applied; ambiguous and high-risk cases remain in faculty review.

Faculty actions and audit entries are stored in this browser using local storage. Export the audit log before clearing browser data.

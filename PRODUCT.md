# PRODUCT.md — Acadza Report → Fix

## Who is most harmed?

The student who trusts the question, spends scarce preparation time solving it, and is then marked wrong or taught a false method is harmed most. The damage is multiplied when the same broken item is served to thousands of learners. The console therefore prioritises answer-key, missing-data, and invalid-question risks; groups duplicate reports; and shows the original stem, solution, evidence and proposed diff together so faculty can decide quickly.

## What should never be auto-applied?

Never auto-author a replacement distractor, reconstruct a missing diagram, declare a question in/out of syllabus, or reassign its subject/chapter without faculty confirmation. These decisions require subject expertise or source material that a report and a text-only record may not contain. The taxonomy already marks these repair types `auto: false`; the product treats that as a hard guard, not a confidence threshold.

## Week-one metric

**Verified harmful-question exposure time:** median time from the first credible report on a genuinely defective question to a faculty-verified correction or quarantine. This measures whether the system reduces the time students continue encountering a harmful item, not just how many reports it processes.

## One idea beyond the brief

Add an **impact-aware quarantine queue**. When a credible answer-key or missing-data defect is detected, estimate how often the question has been served recently and alert the content lead. Faculty can temporarily suppress a high-risk question while the permanent correction is reviewed, with a clear audit trail and rollback.
